My Journey into Research

November 10, 2025

Pursuing a Ph.D. in computer science has been one of the most challenging and rewarding experiences of my life. In this post, I want to share my journey, the lessons I've learned, and some advice for those considering a similar path.

The Beginning

My interest in research started during my undergraduate studies when I took a course on machine learning. I was fascinated by how algorithms could learn patterns from data and make predictions. This curiosity led me to pursue research opportunities and eventually apply for graduate school.

Challenges and Growth

Research is not a linear path. There are moments of frustration when experiments don't work, papers get rejected, and progress feels slow. But these challenges have taught me resilience, critical thinking, and the importance of collaboration. Working with brilliant advisors and peers has been invaluable.

Finding Your Passion

One of the most important lessons is to work on problems you're genuinely passionate about. Research requires sustained effort over long periods, and passion is what keeps you motivated through the difficult times. Don't be afraid to explore different areas until you find what truly excites you.

Advice for Aspiring Researchers

If you're considering research, start by getting involved in undergraduate research projects. Read papers in areas that interest you, attend conferences, and connect with researchers in your field. Most importantly, be patient with yourself and enjoy the journey of discovery.
