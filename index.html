I'm a Computing Science Honors student at the University of Alberta, specializing in Artificial Intelligence. My journey into tech didn't start with a screen, but with a pile of plastic bricks.

From LEGOs to Algorithms

My fascination with programming was sparked by the world of LEGO MINDSTORMS. Competing in the FIRST LEGO League throughout high school taught me more than just how to build robots; it ignited a curiosity for how my code can be translated into machine movements. That same spark drives my work today, as I trade plastic gears for neural networks.

Research & Impact

During my time at the university, I've had the opportunity to dive deep into impactful research projects. I thrive on the challenge of taking complex, theoretical concepts and applying them to solve real-world problems.
